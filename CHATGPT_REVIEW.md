# ChatGPT Review — Key Suggestions (source: shared chat 2026-11-22)

## Perspective
Evaluate as a buy-side equity research analyst. Answer 5 questions: business quality, financial quality, valuation, market confirmation, catalysts/risks.

## 1. Fundamental tab
- Keep all existing metrics.
- ADD: Revenue/PAT/EPS/EBITDA growth YoY, quarterly profit acceleration (prioritise over 3Y CAGR), operating margin trend, 3Y avg ROCE, incremental ROCE >15%, current ratio >1.2, asset turnover.
- ADD valuation: PEG, EV/EBITDA, historical valuation.
- Incremental ROCE is critical — detects loss of competitive advantage.

## 2. Technical tab
- Trend: price vs 50/200 DMA, 50>200 DMA, 200 DMA slope, 20>50 DMA, weekly higher highs/lows, ADX >20 (pref >25), RSI 55–70 (don't auto-penalise >70), MACD histogram.
- Relative strength (CRITICAL): 1M/3M/6M/12M returns vs Nifty, sector-relative, RS acceleration, **RS Rating 0–100** (percentile vs universe).
- Volume/accumulation (MISSING): volume >1.5x on breakout, up-day vs down-day volume, OBV, accumulation/distribution.
- Entry opportunity: distance from 20/50/200 DMA, ATR%, consolidation, breakout confirmation, pullback to support, risk/reward.

## 3. News tab → "News, Events & Market Intelligence"
- Global factors: DXY, USD/INR, US rates, US inflation, crude, gold, copper, gas, China PMI, repo rate (avoid simplistic mappings).
- Domestic: RBI, CPI, WPI, IIP, GST, PMI, credit growth, govt capex, fiscal deficit, monsoon.
- Company-specific news (BIGGEST GAP): results, guidance revisions, order wins/cancellations, capacity expansion, promoter stake sale, FII/DII accumulation, management changes, regulatory action, M&A, rating changes, dividend/buyback.
- News scoring: sentiment (-100..+100), financial materiality (0–100), source confidence (0–100), impact horizon, novelty/surprise. Duplicate detection.

## 4. NEW 4th tab: Investment Decision (most important)
- Do NOT average the 3 scores. Two-stage engine:
  - Stage 1 Eligibility (hard checks): accounting irregularities, auditor resignation, regulatory/governance concerns, persistent negative OCF, excessive leverage, earnings deterioration, extreme valuation, insufficient liquidity. Missing data = UNKNOWN, not pass.
  - Stage 2 Attractiveness (100 pts): Fundamental + Technical + News/catalysts + Risk suitability.
- Classification: 85–100 Strong Buy, 75–84 Buy, 65–74 Watchlist, 50–64 Neutral, <50 Avoid.
- Minimum conditions: Fundamental ≥70, Technical ≥65 for momentum entry, no critical red flags, acceptable valuation.
- Stock research card with thesis + "what could invalidate the thesis".
- Lists: top 25, improving fundamentals, improving technicals, positive catalysts, all-3-agree, deteriorating fundamentals vs bullish charts, strong fundamentals + good entry, critical risk flags.

## 5. Additional metrics
- Earnings surprise/revisions (actual vs consensus).
- Sector strength: 1M/3M/6M returns, RS vs Nifty, breadth (% above 50/200 DMA), earnings growth, valuation percentile.
- Institutional accumulation: FII/DII/MF ownership change, delivery % (note: quarterly data is delayed).
- Risk: beta, ATR%, 1Y volatility, max drawdown, Sharpe, Sortino, avg traded value, correlation with Nifty, sector concentration.

## 6. Three strategies (not one universal BUY score)
- Quality Growth: F≥80, T≥60, earnings≥15%, ROCE≥18%, reasonable valuation.
- Growth Momentum: F≥70, T≥80, RS≥80, positive earnings acceleration, strong volume.
- Value + Turnaround: improving quality, below-history valuation, earnings turnaround, technical reversal, positive catalysts.

## 7. Implementation priority
P0: quarterly earnings growth/acceleration; PEG/historical valuation/EV-EBITDA; RS vs Nifty & sector; volume/delivery; promoter/FII/DII holdings; company-specific news.
P1: earnings surprise/revisions; sector strength/breadth; cash flow & accrual analysis; risk/drawdown.
P2: intrinsic value; portfolio optimisation.
